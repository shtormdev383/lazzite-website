About LAZZITE

LAZZITE OS is an independent Linux distribution created by shtorm. It combines the stability of Debian 13 with the flexibility of KDE Plasma, with one goal: a system that is fast, clean and entirely yours.

Development happens in the open. Follow progress, explore the source and share ideas in the GitHub repository.
